Understanding the Parameters
  • Ion Energy: Higher energy increases sputter yield but may cause more damage
  • Ion Flux: Controls the directional (anisotropic) etch component
  • Angular Spread: Wider spread causes more tapered sidewalls
  • Chemical Rate: Controls the isotropic etch component (undercutting)
  • Mask Selectivity: How much faster substrate etches vs mask
  • Redeposition Rate: Controls sidewall taper from redeposited material
  • Variable Widths: Creates features of 10/50/150 nm to demonstrate microloading (narrow features etch much slower)
