Understanding OES Endpoint Detection
  • Optical Emission Spectroscopy (OES): Monitors light emitted by excited species in the plasma to track etch progress in real-time.
  • Reactant Signal (green): Emission from etchant species (e.g., F atoms). Decreases during etching as reactants are consumed, increases at endpoint when no more material to etch.
  • Product Signal (red): Emission from etch products (e.g., SiF*). Increases during etching, drops sharply at endpoint when layer is cleared.
  • Endpoint Detection: The characteristic change in emission signals indicates the etch has reached the underlying layer - critical for process control.
  • Selectivity: Different materials etch at different rates. OES helps stop precisely at interfaces.
